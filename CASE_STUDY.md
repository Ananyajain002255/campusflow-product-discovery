
# CampusFlow: Product Discovery Case Study

## 1. Problem
College students may have assignments from multiple subjects with different deadlines. When task information is spread across different places, it may be difficult to identify what needs attention first.

## 2. Problem Hypothesis
We believe that displaying assignments, deadlines, and priorities in one simple dashboard will help students identify their next task more easily.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a confirmed user finding.

## 3. Target Users
College students who manage coursework and assignments across multiple subjects.

## 4. Research Approach
Secondary research on assignment submission and student time management was used to motivate the problem. Direct interviews and prototype testing are required to validate the problem with the target users.

## 5. Proposed Solution
CampusFlow is a lightweight assignment tracker featuring:
- A dashboard with assignment statistics
- An assignment creation form
- Deadline and priority labels
- Search and subject filters
- Completion tracking

## 6. Prototype
Built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Browser local storage is used to retain assignment data on the same browser.

## 7. Success Metrics
Metric 1: Median time taken to identify the next due assignment. Proposed target: 25% less time than the participant's current method.

Metric 2: Unassisted completion rate for adding and completing assignments. Proposed target: At least 4 out of 5 test users complete the task without help.

## 8. Limitations
The current prototype uses browser-based storage and sample assignments. It does not include user accounts, cloud synchronization, or college learning-management-system integration.

## 9. Next Steps
1. Test the prototype with 3-5 college students.
2. Record task completion, time, confusion, and feedback.
3. Identify usability issues.
4. Improve the prototype based on actual evidence.

## 10. Conclusion
CampusFlow explores a simple way to organize student assignments. User testing will determine whether the dashboard genuinely helps students manage deadlines more efficiently.
